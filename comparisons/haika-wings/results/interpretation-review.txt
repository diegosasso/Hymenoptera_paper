Independent interpretation review, 2026-11-20

Defensible main conclusion
-------------------------
This analysis does not establish reproducible positive concordance between the
ontophylo forewing rate reconstruction and the reconstructed HAIKA family-profile
rates. The primary 77-tip fit has weak branch association and a different temporal
profile, but temporal direction and some branch associations change among ANN
initializations and modeling/sampling sensitivities. It is therefore premature
to interpret the result as a robust biological conflict between the methods.

Suggested short results wording
------------------------------
"Using the same dated tree and an explicit family-level alignment, the primary
HAIKA reconstruction showed little correspondence with ontophylo forewing branch
rates (Spearman rho = 0.068 across 152 branches; rho = 0.083 across 50 branches
subtending multiple families). Neither association exceeded the positive
concordance expected under the conditional homogeneous-BM/proxy null (upper-tail
probabilities 0.895 and 0.955). Relative marginal rates were negatively associated
in the primary 77-tip analysis (rho = -0.463 across 28 full-width time bins), but
this temporal result was sensitive to ANN initialization and family-representative
sampling. These conditional comparisons provide no consistent rate concordance;
they do not test the general validity of image-derived morphology or ontophylo."

--------------------------

These empirical probabilities use 199 validated null replicates and the +1
correction. The two-tail statistic doubles the smaller empirical tail. They are
unadjusted exploratory probabilities under a fitted homogeneous multivariate-BM
null, conditional on the tree, retained PCA coordinates, family assignment and
ontophylo summary. They are not a general test of independence between arbitrary
heterogeneous rate histories, a joint posterior probability, or a test of ANN
accuracy. Shared phylogenetic geometry and proxy copying are retained by the null.

The 77-tip temporal result is relatively unusual under that particular null:
its observed rho is -0.4635, compared with null median 0.6611 and central 95%
range [-0.2173, 0.8673]. However, the 48-tip representative analysis has null
median -0.4407 and central range [-0.6758, 0.5059]; its observed rho=-0.5175 is
ordinary under this alternative sampling design. The change in the null itself
shows why a single negative correlation is insufficient evidence for biological
decoupling. Do not describe the 0.02 result as a sampling-robust discovery.

Temporal comparison
-------------------
In the primary 77-tip fit, ontophylo's largest full-bin mean is at 230-240 Ma:
6.50 times its own lineage-duration-weighted treewide mean, with map quantiles
4.97-8.05. HAIKA is 0.65 times its own mean in that bin. Its largest full-bin
value is 1.35 at 240-250 Ma. Thus HAIKA does not reproduce the pronounced primary
ontophylo peak in the same bin. This does not establish a precisely dated lag
or a difference in absolute biological rates: the methods measure different
quantities, and branch rates have been averaged into 10-Myr bins.

Across the five ANN seeds at lambda=1, 77-tip temporal rho ranges from -0.4635
to +0.1963 (seed values: -0.4635, +0.0851, -0.0370, +0.1963, -0.3430). Therefore
"all HAIKA models show the opposite history" would be false. Across those same
seeds, all-branch rho ranges from 0.0523 to 0.2154, while interfamily rho ranges
from 0.0829 to 0.4821. Some embeddings show moderate interfamily association;
only seed3407 has the present conditional-null calibration.

Other completed sensitivities
-----------------------------
For seed3407 full77, changing lambda from 0.1 to 1 to 10 gives temporal rho
-0.1235, -0.4635 and -0.6200, and all-branch rho 0.0771, 0.0679 and -0.0418.
The 48 first/last representative choices at lambda=1 give temporal rho -0.5175
and -0.3391. Original-segment versus contracted-branch marginalization also
changes the 48-tip temporal result; these are separately exported estimands.

Removing the forewing muscle character produces an eight-character sensitivity
with all-branch rho=0.1566, interfamily rho=0.0902, and time rho=-0.4552 in the
primary 77-tip fit. The principal qualitative result is therefore not solely
caused by the muscle character, although character representation still matters.

All 18 additional bound/stronger-smoothing fits passed convergence and likelihood
checks. At the default lower offset of 10 log units, the selected 77-tip fits
have 184 lower-bound node-by-axis parameters out of 459; the 48-tip fits have
11 out of 285. These count parameters in the selected fits across three axes,
not unique branches or repeated optimizer starts. All bound gradients point
toward still smaller unconstrained rates. Optimizer convergence consequently
does not establish uniquely identified biological rates.

Moving the lower offset to 5 or 15 preserves most TOTAL_VARIANCE branch rankings.
For 77 tips, rank agreement with the default is 0.9713 and 0.9747, and correlation
with ontophylo changes from 0.0679 to 0.0919 and 0.0599. For 48 tips, agreement is
0.9956 and 0.9996, with ontophylo correlation 0.1746 and 0.1645 rather than 0.1702.
The broader conclusion of weak branch concordance therefore persists in this
bound range. It does not mean the lowest rates are resolved: even offset 15
leaves 174 selected bound parameters in the 77-tip fits and one in the 48-tip
fits. These are explicitly constrained rate estimates.

The stronger smoothing sensitivity (lambda equal to root age, 280.0259) removes
all bound hits but substantially changes detailed branch rankings: agreement
with the default is only 0.4794 for 77 tips and 0.4030 for 48 tips. Ontophylo
branch correlations become -0.1184 and -0.0862, respectively; these remain weak
and do not establish positive concordance. The corresponding temporal
correlations are -0.6534 and -0.5684. Thus the broad absence of consistent positive
concordance survives the tested bound/smoothing alternatives, while precise
branch-rate order is sensitive to smoothing. Stronger smoothing is a declared
sensitivity, not an established preferable model; no new conditional-null
probabilities were calibrated for it. The lambda=1 null probabilities above
must not be transferred to these alternative fits.

What is actually being compared
------------------------------
The authorized 77-tip alignment uses 48 family profiles derived from 112 source
images. Forty-one families have one source image. It is not a 77-species matched
measurement dataset; multiple study tips receive the same family profile. The
48-tip analyses select one study representative per family and reduce this
duplication, but do not supply missing within-family measurements. Three repeated
families are nonmonophyletic on the tree, so selecting representatives is not
equivalent to collapsing 48 monophyletic clades.

The usable seed matrices are 64-dimensional archived embeddings. The comparison
uses new centered, unscaled PCA of family means, retaining at least two axes and
at least 90% variance. For seed3407 this is three axes and 96.94% variance.
These new axes must not be labeled as the publication's identified "shape" and
"venation" PC1/PC2. Provenance mismatches and duplicate profiles in the supplied
Case3/published-PC products explain why those products were not used as direct
species-level measurements; they do not establish their authors' intent or the
cause of the discrepancies.

Ontophylo measures mapped discrete-state transition counts divided by duration.
HAIKA BM measures continuous-feature variance per duration. TOTAL_VARIANCE sums
the separate fitted PC variance rates before normalization; it is an exploratory
retained-feature trace, not a jointly fitted multivariate posterior. Dimensionless
normalization makes relative patterns comparable without making absolute units
equivalent.

Anatomical correction
--------------------
The final character audit supersedes an earlier preliminary description in this
reviewer's methods notes. CH353-CH359 describe seven forewing vein/stigma traits,
CH360 describes nygmata, and CH294 describes a muscle. There is substantial
anatomical overlap with HAIKA's forewing outline/venation images. "Sclerite" is
the broad source ontology type for the eight nonmuscle characters, not evidence
that they predominantly describe wing-base sclerites. The relevant differences
are discrete states versus continuous image geometry, representation weighting,
the muscle/nygmata content, and proxy versus exemplar sampling.

Uncertainty and claims to avoid
------------------------------
The 1,000-map intervals describe ontophylo reconstruction-map variation conditional
on its fixed model parameters and the HAIKA point estimate. They are not joint
confidence/credible intervals on the cross-method association. In particular,
the correlation of mean rates need not lie inside quantiles of correlations
computed separately for each map; these are different nonlinear summaries.

Avoid claims that HAIKA is inaccurate, that ontophylo is validated by disagreement,
that all model variants yield negative correspondence, that weak association
proves evolutionary independence, or that family duplication represents measured
within-family stasis. The supported conclusion is limited concordance in the
primary conditional comparison and substantial sensitivity in broader claims.
