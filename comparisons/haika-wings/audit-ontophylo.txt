ONTOPHYLO WING RECONSTRUCTION AUDIT
Date: 2026-11-20

Source and tree
---------------
Repository: https://github.com/diegosasso/Hymenoptera_paper
Source HEAD: c22dfaa65726162f2e3e6100149a4f5acc3de46e (2026-11-18)
Local ontophylo package source HEAD: d1e6e05108cc243f0b8d317edc2fc2328fcde9f9
Extraction uses base R and ape; it does not depend on the current ontophylo
package implementation or refit the original character models.

The dated tree has 77 tips, 76 internal nodes and 152 edges. Its root age is
280.0259 Ma and total lineage duration is approximately 10798.53 Myr. Original
branch lengths are preserved. Terminal depths differ by at most 1.1e-7 Myr,
which is rounding error under the source's ultrametric-tree interpretation.
The pointwise curve explicitly includes every terminal branch at age zero.

There are two distinct taxon label sets:
  data/hym_tree.RDS: molecular exemplars from Blaimer et al. (2023).
  data_out/paramo_stm_adult_final.RDA, hym_tree: morphology genera from Sharkey.
They have identical edge matrices and branch lengths. Of the 77 tips, 22
have different morphology and molecular genus labels. Examples include
Psilocharis morphology on a molecular Eucharis position and Macroxyela
morphology on a molecular Xyela position. Other differences may involve
taxonomic nomenclature; the export flags literal differences without
asserting that each is a biological substitution. Neither label set should
be silently treated as a species-level HAIKA match. Exact morphology-genus,
exact molecular-species, molecular-genus and family proxies must be recorded
separately if used.

tree-tip-metadata.csv preserves the source taxon mapping. tree-branch-geometry.csv
preserves edge numbers (BR_1 through BR_152), parent/child IDs, older/younger
ages, and sorted descendant sets for stable clade matching. Use the RDS tree
when aligning existing edge rows; Newick readers may reorder edges.

Wing data and scope
-------------------
Fore wing: 9 characters, CH294 and CH353 through CH360.
  CH294 is a muscle character. The remaining eight carry the source's
  "sclerite" classification and describe wing veins, stigma and nygmata.
Hind wing: 10 characters, CH210, CH322, CH363, CH365, CH366, CH367, CH368,
  CH370, CH371, CH372. CH322 is a muscle character.
All wings: union of the above, 19 characters; the partitions do not overlap.

The original source also contains CH364 among seven excluded high-rate
characters. This extraction retains the final published-analysis character
selection rather than silently restoring excluded data.

Outputs include all original partitions and fore/hind sclerite-only
sensitivities (8 and 9 characters respectively). "Sclerite" is the original
annotation class, not a claim that every character is visible in an ordinary
wing image. All-wing comparison is anatomically broader than fore-wing
image-only measurements. The fore-wing and fore-wing-sclerite comparisons
therefore provide important scope checks if HAIKA uses fore wings only.

Existing rate estimand
---------------------
For character c, branch b and stochastic-map sample s:
  N[b,c,s] = number of mapped state transitions on the entire branch.
For an anatomical partition C:
  r[b,s] = sum over c in C of N[b,c,s] / branch_length[b].
Units are summed reconstructed character-state transitions per Myr.
These are realized-history summaries under fitted discrete-state models,
not independently estimated branch-specific Q parameters or BM variances.
Dividing by the number of characters gives mean changes per character per
Myr, but still does not put them on the same absolute scale as BM diffusion.

There are 1,000 maps per character. Every exported character/map has checked
edge order, branch lengths and tip labels against the reference tree.
Re-extracted fore-wing and hind-wing matrices reproduce the stored
data_out/br_rates_all.RDS values with maximum absolute errors 5.55e-17 and
1.39e-17 respectively. Stored branch means are reproduced to <4e-15.
The count array retains all individual character samples for later
repartitioning and for matching/pruning sensitivity analyses.

Source R/helpers.R selects ER, SYM or ARD by AICc for independent characters
and supplies an explicit Q for dependent characters. It obtains root priors
using maddfitz and passes fixed Q_matrix, root_prior and tips_prior to
phytools::make.simmap. The saved best_model objects have class raydisc, while
the current helper calls corHMM and retains a commented rayDISC call. Thus
the current script alone does not prove the historical fitting engine or
its version. The exported fitted model objects preserve the actual Q and
prior values used for the saved reconstruction.

Intervals are empirical 2.5% and 97.5% quantiles across stochastic maps.
They condition on fitted character models, their root/tip priors, the tree,
taxon mapping and selected characters. They are not confidence intervals
for fully estimated evolutionary-rate parameters and do not include tree,
Q/model-selection or proxy-taxon uncertainty. Maps for separate characters
were sampled separately; equal sample indices are combined as independent
reconstruction draws, not a jointly fitted multicharacter posterior.

The data_nhpp/adult_body_reg_wings.RDS and fore/hind counterparts contain a
different, smoothed within-branch NHPP/KDE reconstruction based on 100
subsampled maps and discretization. They are available as secondary data,
but are not substituted for the 1,000-map branch-count estimates here.

Marginal curves and pruning
--------------------------
Pointwise rate at age t is the unweighted mean of r[b,s] over the branches
crossing t. Thus it represents a sampled-lineage mean at that age, not the
sum of rates or the rate for a randomly selected terminal tip. The export
uses 1,000 ages between zero and the original root age. Exact node-age
conventions affect isolated endpoints only; root age uses outgoing edges.

For time bin [a,z], branch exposure is
  L[b,a,z] = max(0, min(older_age[b],z) - max(younger_age[b],a)).
The binned rate is
  sum_b L[b,a,z] * r[b,s] / sum_b L[b,a,z].
The exported 10-Myr bins use exact branch overlap; the final bin is the
short 280 to 280.0259 Ma interval and should be excluded or combined with
its neighbor before statistical summaries. It is retained transparently.
Total bin exposure reproduces total tree length within 5.5e-12 Myr.

For a common-taxon tree, never match branches by row number after pruning.
Use descendant clades and explicit original-edge paths. If a pruned edge
contracts original edges b1,...,bk, its mean rate is
  sum_i r[bi,s] * length[bi] / sum_i length[bi],
equivalently the summed transition count divided by summed duration.
An unweighted average of component rates is incorrect.

This projection is the existing full-tree reconstruction restricted to
retained lineages. It is not equivalent to fitting/reconstructing after
dropping taxa. A matched-tip refit/remapping is a useful sensitivity test
for unequal information. A second temporal issue remains: spreading a
contracted edge's average rate over its entire duration erases the original
within-path changes in rate. For matched marginal curves, retain original
edge segments and ages along the surviving paths, or explicitly label a
comparison in which both methods are averaged uniformly over contracted
edges. Preserve the original root age and state whether stem segments
above the retained taxa's MRCA are excluded.

Comparison to BM
----------------
Direct equality of raw discrete transition rates and squared ANN-feature
units per Myr is not meaningful. Compare relative branch profiles, ranks,
and temporal shape with the same taxon/edge support and weighting. Normalize
each reconstruction draw by its lineage-duration-weighted treewide mean
when dimensionless rates are needed. Both pipelines share the same tree,
so naive correlations/P values treating edges or adjacent time points as
independent observations are not a phylogenetic test. BM rate estimation
uncertainty is also distinct from stochastic-map history uncertainty.

Independent HAIKA data review addendum
-------------------------------------
I independently compared the extracted Case3 and Case2 numerical vectors
across all 28 feature columns. The following exact identities are confirmed:
  Tetragonula_carbonaria -> Apocrita-Trigonalidae
  Osmia_cornuta -> Aculeata-Osirini_Osiris
  Heriades_truncorum -> Aculeata-Osirini_Osiris
The first crosses the bee/Trigonalidae boundary and the latter two cross
the Megachilidae/Apidae boundary. These identities do not establish their
cause, but require a verified image-to-vector-to-taxon mapping before
species-level inference. They cannot be explained merely as alternative
spellings of the same taxa.

The S4 64-feature phylogenetic matrix contains 61 named taxa but only 26
unique vectors. Repeated rows systematically span entire families: all
13 Tenthredinidae labels have identical vectors, as do both Xyelidae genera,
five Pergidae genera, five Pamphiliidae genera, three Cephidae genera and
four Siricidae genera. A match of the genus string in S4 therefore does not
establish an independently measured image for that genus. Treating these
as genus measurements would impose zero observed within-family divergence.

The HAIKA extraction agent's PCA diagnostic reports that ordinary centered
or standardized PCA on the supplied Case3 28-column matrix does not recover
the published PC1/PC2 values (absolute same-axis correlations 0.43-0.61).
The matrix gives different explained-variance fractions as well. The
published analysis may have used another feature version or preprocessing;
the discrepancy alone does not identify an error. Until reconciled, the
published PC coordinates and supplied feature matrix should be treated as
different, incompletely documented data products. Copying the PC scores
does not solve their underlying taxon/proxy provenance.

The original Case2 405-row matrix offers a defensible coarse alternative.
Literal family labels cover all 48 historical Family_Sharkey categories
in the 77-tip mapping. This is coverage at family level, not evidence that
each of the 77 genera has been measured. Many families have a single source
row, while Braconidae and Ichneumonidae have multiple subfamily rows.

Recommended design hierarchy:
1. For a primary 77-tip comparison, obtain images or verified genus-level
   feature vectors for the actual morphology exemplars, with model/feature
   provenance. The present files do not establish that data coverage.
2. An explicitly exploratory family-level comparison can use the original
   Case2 source rows on an induced tree with one documented representative
   per family. Specify whether a single image or a centroid across source
   images/subfamilies represents each family, and check alternative
   representatives. Do not copy one family vector onto all 77 tips.
3. An S4 matched-genus or Case3/published-PC analysis can be a reproduction
   or sensitivity exercise, clearly retaining the source's duplicated/proxy
   values. It is unsuitable as independent evidence of genus-specific
   rates until the image-to-taxon mapping is resolved.

Projection helper
-----------------
scripts/project-ontophylo.R implements project_ontophylo(full, keep).
It returns the induced tree, clade-key branch geometry, original edge paths,
contracted count/rate samples, retained and excluded stem edges, and exact
10-Myr marginal samples under both original-segment and contracted-constant
interpretations. Its time grid retains the original 280.0259 Ma endpoint;
bins wholly above the retained MRCA have zero exposure and NA rates.
The retained MRCA age is given in original absolute Ma, independently of
the induced tree's root depth.

Light checks used five taxa across the tree (Nasonia, Orussus, Xyela,
Macroxyela, Tenthredo), three chalcidoid taxa (Nasonia, Cleonymus,
Megastigmus), and all 77 taxa. Contracted lengths and transition totals
were conserved to numerical precision; the maximum marginal count error
in the five-tip case was 2.5e-14. The chalcidoid case correctly excluded
17 stem edges and retained the original MRCA age of 112.0643 Ma. The all-tip
identity case exactly reproduced the original fore-wing rate samples.
