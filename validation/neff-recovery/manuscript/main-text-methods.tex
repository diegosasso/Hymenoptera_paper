\subsection*{Simulation-based recovery of phenomic diversity}
\phantomsection
\label{sec:neff-main}

We tested whether the reconstruction pipeline recovers a known association
between accelerated phenomic diversity ($N_{\mathrm{eff}}^{+}$) and lineage
diversification. We generated 100 independent trees with a 100-Myr interval
of elevated speciation and imposed character origins on ten contemporaneous
branches within that interval. Each branch represented a different anatomical
unit comprising 20 binary characters. Characters originated in state~1 and
subsequently evolved slowly, allowing occasional losses and regains.
Using only the simulated tree and terminal character states, we reconstructed
character histories with \texttt{ontophylo} and compared the resulting
$N_{\mathrm{eff}}^{+}$ trajectories with those calculated from the actual
simulated histories. We used strict upper-tail branch-rate classifications
to prevent zero-rate ties from being labelled accelerated, and examined the
inclusive convention separately. The known generating diversification rate was used as
the reference; diversification parameters were not re-estimated.
Detailed procedures and diagnostic figures are provided in
\hyperref[sec:neff-supplement]{Supplementary Methods}.

Across the 100 trials, the median correlation between true and reconstructed
$N_{\mathrm{eff}}^{+}$ trajectories was \NeffRecoveryR{}, with a median
root-mean-square error of \NeffRecoveryRMSE{}.
Positive correlations between reconstructed $N_{\mathrm{eff}}^{+}$ and
known diversification met the nominal two-sided $p<0.05$ threshold in
\NeffDetected{} of 100 trials (\NeffDetectedPercent{}\%; exact 95\%
binomial interval, \NeffDetectedLower{}--\NeffDetectedUpper{}\%).
The corresponding frequency for $N_{\mathrm{eff}}^{+}$ calculated from
true histories was \NeffTruthDetected{} of 100 trials
(Figures~\ref{fig:neff-recovery} and~\ref{fig:neff-detection}).
Reconstruction retained all \NeffBothDetected{} true-history detections and yielded \NeffAdditionalDetected{}
additional nominal detections, showing that accurate recovery of trajectory
shape does not guarantee identical significance. The inclusive-cutoff
variant produced constant diversity in all trials, so its correlations
were undefined; the recovery results above refer to the strict definition.

This experiment evaluates recovery under an imposed association and the
specified tree and character-generating processes. The detection frequencies
are based on the manuscript's Pearson comparison of matched 20-Myr bins;
they do not adjust for temporal autocorrelation. They therefore describe
nominal detection under this design, rather than power calibrated against a
null false-positive rate. Recovery of this positive control does not establish
that phenomic diversification caused the empirical diversification pattern.
