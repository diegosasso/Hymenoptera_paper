\paragraph{Paired simulation results.}
The reconstructed metric showed positive correlations with diversification
at nominal two-sided $p<0.05$ in 78 of 100 associated trials, compared with
0 of 100 homogeneous-ER controls
(0\%; exact 95\% binomial interval,
0.0--3.6\%).
No reconstructed control correlation was significant in either direction.
Using exact simulated histories, 4 of 100 controls met the
$p<0.05$ threshold; all were positive (Table~\ref{tab:paired-neff}).
Median true--reconstructed trajectory correlations were 0.996 in the
associated scenario and 0.955 in the control; median
root-mean-square errors were 0.089 and 0.288, respectively.
These results evaluate recovery and specificity conditional on the simulated
tree ensemble and character generators. They do not demonstrate causality
or guarantee calibration for the empirical reconstructed time series.
