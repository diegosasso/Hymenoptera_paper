\noindent\textbf{Randomization test of the $N_{\mathrm{eff}}^{+}$--diversification association.}
Accelerated-branch assignments were independently shuffled within 15 anatomical units on the fixed 77-tip tree, preserving per-unit counts (9,999 randomizations).
\textbf{a}, Randomized $N_{\mathrm{eff}}^{+}$ trajectories (grey), their arithmetic mean (orange), and the observed trajectory (green), each averaged across 1,000 reconstruction samples.
\textbf{b}, Pearson correlations with PyRate net diversification across thirteen 20-Myr bins (260--0 Ma). The observed correlation ($r=0.950$) exceeded all randomized values (one-sided permutation $p=0.0001$).
\textbf{c}, Nominal two-sided Pearson $p$ values, shown as $-\log_{10}(p)$; the dashed line marks $p=0.05$. Green lines in \textbf{b,c} indicate observed values. Although 6.58\% of randomizations yielded $p<0.05$, all had negative correlations; none produced a significant positive association.
